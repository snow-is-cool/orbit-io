HOW TO PLAY

  • Move your mouse to guide your planet through space.
  • Absorb smaller particles and stardust to increase your mass.
  • If you are less than 10% of another planet's mass, you will be caught in its orbit!
  • If you 10x the mass of another planet, you can capture them in your orbit!
  • If you are trapped, click rapidly to push yourself out from a gravity well.
  • Avoid larger planets that can shatter you on contact.
  • Hold left click to eject mass and gain a speed boost. Requires 5 stardust/planets in orbit.
  • Scroll or use +/- buttons to zoom the camera.
  • Mobile: Drag to move your planet. Tap the BURST button to eject mass (when you have 5+ in orbit). Tap rapidly when captured to escape.
